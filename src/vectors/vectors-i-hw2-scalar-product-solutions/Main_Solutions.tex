\Q{\textcolor{black!60}{\small[TJC 2025 Prelim P1 Q6]}\par\smallskip\textit{Official solution.}}
\par\smallskip\noindent\textbf{(a)}\par
Using the ratio theorem, $\vv{OX}=\dfrac{3\mathbf a+\mathbf b}{4}$.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textbf{(b)}\par
$\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=|\mathbf a||\mathbf b|\cos\dfrac{\pi}{6}=\lambda\cdot\lambda\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\lambda^2$.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textbf{(c)}\par
\begin{align*}
\vv{OY}&=\left(\vv{OX}\cdot\hat{\mathbf b}\right)\hat{\mathbf b}
=\left(\frac{3\mathbf a+\mathbf b}{4}\cdot\frac{\mathbf b}{|\mathbf b|}\right)\frac{\mathbf b}{|\mathbf b|}
=\frac{1}{4|\mathbf b|^2}(3\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b+\mathbf b\cdot\mathbf b)\,\mathbf b\\
&=\frac{1}{4\lambda^2}\left(3\lambda^2\cos\frac{\pi}{6}+\lambda^2\right)\mathbf b
=\frac{1}{4}\left(3\cdot\frac{\sqrt3}{2}+1\right)\mathbf b
=\frac{2+3\sqrt3}{8}\mathbf b\quad\text{(shown)}.
\end{align*}
\par\smallskip\noindent\textbf{(d)}\par
\begin{align*}
\text{Area of }\Delta OXY
&=\frac12\left|\vv{OX}\times\vv{OY}\right|
=\frac12\left|\frac{3\mathbf a+\mathbf b}{4}\times\frac{2+3\sqrt3}{8}\mathbf b\right|
=\frac{3(2+3\sqrt3)}{64}\left|\mathbf a\times\mathbf b\right|\\
&=\frac{3(2+3\sqrt3)}{64}|\mathbf a||\mathbf b|\sin\frac{\pi}{6}
=\frac{3(2+3\sqrt3)}{64}\lambda\cdot\lambda\cdot\frac12
=\frac{3(2+3\sqrt3)}{128}\lambda^2\text{ units}^2.
\end{align*}
\Q{\textcolor{black!60}{\small[HCI 2025 Prelim P2 Q4]}\par\smallskip\textit{Official solution.}}
\par\smallskip\noindent\textbf{(a)(i)}\par
$\mathbf c=-\mathbf a-\mathbf b$.
\begin{align*}
\mathbf c\cdot\mathbf c&=(\mathbf a+\mathbf b)\cdot(\mathbf a+\mathbf b)=|\mathbf a|^2+2\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b+|\mathbf b|^2\\
1&=1+2\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b+1\qquad\Rightarrow\qquad\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=-\frac12.
\end{align*}
\par\smallskip\noindent\textbf{(a)(ii)}\par
$-\dfrac12=|\mathbf a||\mathbf b|\cos\angle AOB\Rightarrow\cos\angle AOB=-\dfrac12\Rightarrow\angle AOB=120^\circ$.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textbf{(a)(iii)}\par
Since $\angle AOB=120^\circ$ and $OA=OB=OC$, the triangles $OAB$, $OAC$ and $OBC$ are congruent and $\angle ACB=\angle ABC=\angle BCA=60^\circ$. Hence triangle $ABC$ is an equilateral triangle.

Since $\vv{OD}=\mathbf a+\mathbf b=-\mathbf c$ and $OD$, $AB$ are diagonals of the rhombus $OADB$, $OD\perp AB$. Hence $ACBD$ is a kite.
Official Method 1:
\begin{align*}
\text{Area of }ACBD&=2\cdot\tfrac12\left|\vv{AC}\times\vv{DC}\right|

\Q{\textcolor{black!60}{\small[SAJC 2025 Prelim P1 Q11]}\par\smallskip\textit{Official solution.}}
\par\smallskip\noindent\textbf{(i)}\par
Since $AA'$ is a straight line that passes through $O$, $\vv{OA'}=-\mathbf a$.
\begin{align*}
\vv{BA}&=\mathbf a-\mathbf b,\qquad \vv{BA'}=-\mathbf a-\mathbf b\\
\vv{BA}\cdot\vv{BA'}&=(\mathbf a-\mathbf b)\cdot(-\mathbf a-\mathbf b)
=-|\mathbf a|^2-\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b+\mathbf b\cdot\mathbf a+|\mathbf b|^2\\
&=-|\mathbf a|^2+|\mathbf b|^2=0\quad\text{(since $|\mathbf a|=|\mathbf b|=$ radius)}.
\end{align*}
Hence $\vv{BA}\perp\vv{BA'}$, so $\angle ABA'=90^\circ$ (shown).
\par\smallskip\noindent\textbf{(ii)}\par
Area of $\Delta OAB=\dfrac12|\mathbf a\times\mathbf b|$. From the diagram, $\dfrac12\cdot OA\cdot h=\dfrac12|\mathbf a\times\mathbf b|$.
\begin{align*}
\text{Area of }\Delta ABA'&=\frac12\cdot AA'\cdot h=\frac12\cdot 2\,OA\cdot h
=\left(\frac12\cdot OA\cdot h\right)\cdot 2
=|\mathbf a\times\mathbf b|.
\end{align*}
Thus $k=1$.
