\Q{\textcolor{black!60}{\small[TJC 2025 Prelim P1 Q6]}\par\smallskip
The points $A$ and $B$ lie on a circle with centre $O$ and radius $\lambda$ units. With reference to the origin $O$, points $A$ and $B$ have position vectors $\mathbf a$ and $\mathbf b$ respectively. The point $X$ on the line segment $AB$ is such that $AX:XB=1:3$ and the point $Y$ is the foot of the perpendicular from $X$ to $OB$.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(\alph*)},leftmargin=2.2em,itemsep=3pt]
\item Find the position vector of $X$.\hfill[1]
\item It is given that the acute angle $AOB$ is $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$. Find $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b$ in terms of $\lambda$.\hfill[1]
\item Show that the position vector of $Y$ is $\dfrac{2+3\sqrt3}{8}\mathbf b$.\hfill[2]
\item Hence find the exact area of $\Delta OXY$ in terms of $\lambda$.\hfill[4]
\end{enumerate}}
\Q{\textcolor{black!60}{\small[HCI 2025 Prelim P2 Q4]}\par\smallskip
Let $A$, $B$ and $C$ be the points on the same plane with position vectors $\mathbf a$, $\mathbf b$ and $\mathbf c$ respectively and $\mathbf a+\mathbf b+\mathbf c=\mathbf 0$. It is given that vectors $\mathbf a$, $\mathbf b$ and $\mathbf c$ are unit vectors.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(\alph*)},leftmargin=2.2em,itemsep=3pt]
\item \begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=1.8em,itemsep=2pt]
\item By considering $\mathbf c\cdot\mathbf c$, find the value of $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b$.\hfill[3]
\item Find the angle $\angle AOB$.\hfill[2]
\item Draw the position vectors $\mathbf a$, $\mathbf b$ and $\mathbf c$ on a single diagram. Using your diagram, identify the type of triangle $ABC$.\hfill[2]
\end{enumerate}
\item The point $D$ has position vector $\mathbf a+\mathbf b$. Find the area of the quadrilateral $ACBD$.\hfill[2]
\end{enumerate}}

\Q{\textcolor{black!60}{\small[VJC 2025 Prelim P1 Q3]}\par\smallskip
Referred to the origin $O$, points $A$ and $B$ have position vectors $\mathbf a$ and $\mathbf b$ respectively. Point $C$ lies on $OA$, such that $OC:CA=2:1$. Point $D$ lies on $OB$, such that $OD:DB=\lambda:\mu$. It is given that the area of triangle $ABD$ is half the area of triangle $ABC$.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(\alph*)},leftmargin=2.2em,itemsep=3pt]
\item Show that the area of triangle $ABD$ is given by $\dfrac{\mu}{2(\lambda+\mu)}|\mathbf a\times\mathbf b|$. Hence find the ratio $\lambda:\mu$.\hfill[4]
\item The point $E$ has position vector $\dfrac14\mathbf a+\dfrac58\mathbf b$. Show that $A$, $E$ and $D$ are collinear.\hfill[3]
\end{enumerate}
It is further given that the angle $AOB$ is $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and $O$ lies on the perpendicular bisector of the line segment $AB$.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(\alph*)},leftmargin=2.2em,itemsep=3pt,start=3]
\item Find the length of projection of $\mathbf a$ on $\mathbf b$, giving your answer in terms of $|\mathbf b|$. Hence find the position vector of the point $F$, the foot of the perpendicular from $A$ to $OB$.\hfill[3]
\end{enumerate}}
