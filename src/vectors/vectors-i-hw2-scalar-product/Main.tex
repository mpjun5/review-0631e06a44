\Q{\textcolor{black!60}{\small[EJC 2024 Promo Q5]}\par\smallskip
$\mathbf a$ and $\mathbf b$ are vectors such that $|\mathbf a|=\sqrt{2}$ and $|\mathbf b|=\sqrt{6}$. The angle between $\mathbf a$ and $\mathbf b$ is $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(\alph*)},leftmargin=2.2em,itemsep=3pt]
\item Find the exact length of projection of $\mathbf a$ onto $\mathbf b$.\hfill[2]
\item By considering $(3\mathbf a+2\mathbf b)\cdot(3\mathbf a+2\mathbf b)$, find the exact value of $|3\mathbf a+2\mathbf b|$.\hfill[4]
\end{enumerate}}
\Q{\textcolor{black!60}{\small[SAJC 2024 Promo Q9(a)]}\par\smallskip
Relative to the origin $O$, the points $A$ and $B$ have position vectors $\mathbf a$ and $\mathbf b$ respectively, where $\mathbf a$ and $\mathbf b$ are non-parallel. It is given that $|\mathbf a|=9$, $|\mathbf b|=1$ and $|2\mathbf a+\mathbf b|=2\sqrt{74}$.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(\roman*)},leftmargin=2.2em,itemsep=3pt]
\item By considering $(2\mathbf a+\mathbf b)\cdot(2\mathbf a+\mathbf b)$, show that $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=-\dfrac{29}{4}$.\hfill[3]
\item Give a geometrical meaning of $|\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b|$.\hfill[1]
\item The points $P$, $Q$ and $R$ have position vectors $7\mathbf a-5\mathbf b$, $6\mathbf a+5\mathbf b$ and $9\mathbf a+\lambda\mathbf b$ respectively. Given that these three points are collinear, find the value of $\lambda$.\hfill[3]
\end{enumerate}}
\Q{\textcolor{black!60}{\small[CJC 2022 Promo Q9(i),(ii)]}\par\smallskip
Relative to the origin $O$, the position vectors of points $A$, $B$ and $C$ are $\mathbf a$, $\mathbf b$ and $\mathbf c$ respectively, where $\mathbf b$ is a unit vector. It is given that $\mathbf b$ and $\mathbf b-\mathbf a$ are perpendicular and $C$ lies on $AB$ such that $AC:CB=3:1$.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(\roman*)},leftmargin=2.2em,itemsep=3pt]
\item Show that $|\mathbf a|=\sec\theta$, where $\theta$ is the angle between $\mathbf a$ and $\mathbf b$.\hfill[3]
\end{enumerate}
By expressing $\mathbf c$ in terms of $\mathbf a$ and $\mathbf b$,
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(\roman*)},leftmargin=2.2em,itemsep=3pt,start=2]
\item find the value of $|\mathbf c\cdot\mathbf b|$ and state the geometrical interpretation of $|\mathbf c\cdot\mathbf b|$.\hfill[4]
\end{enumerate}}
\Q{\textcolor{black!60}{\small[SAJC 2025 Promo Q8(i)]}\par\smallskip
Relative to the origin $O$, two points $A$ and $B$ have position vectors $\mathbf a$ and $\mathbf b$ respectively. It is given that $|\mathbf a|=2$, $|\mathbf b|=1$ and $|3\mathbf a-2\mathbf b|=\sqrt{37}$.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(\roman*)},leftmargin=2.2em,itemsep=3pt]

\end{enumerate}}
